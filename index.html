Renewable vs coal, how does cost affect the adoption of renewables?

What's the current situation?

At present, the most common power source in Australia is coal due to the infrastructure that has been built up over many years and the lowered cost as a result.

Coal-fired power stations still support more than 50 per cent of Australia’s electricity but not for much longer as at least half of the remaining coal generation fleet is due to shut within 10 years. Two of the nation’s largest coal plants, Yallourn in Victoria and Eraring in NSW were initially supposed to close in the 2030s, but have made commercial decisions to bring forward their closure dates over the past four years, partly due to the soaring costs of maintaining ageing infrastructure.

What's the cost difference?

According to the CSIRO’s (Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation) annual energy cost report, known as ‘GenCost’. Its latest release, the 2025-26 financial year draft report (December 2025) found that general black coal has a cost of $121 - $195 per Megawatt hour and $52 - $88 for photovoltaic solar. This makes the minimum difference between their costs in $/MWh is $33 in favor of photovoltaic solar being cheaper. This addresses a key concern for the coal industry, with recent government subsidies and social push, rooftop solar has become quite common and with this, coal has been facing a downfall as it’s now being out competed in terms of quantity, speed, price and accessibility.

Conclusion

A key concept these numbers can't fully show is that during sunny days when home rooftop solar majoritively takes over powering houses, the power from the coal stations isn't used as much. As coal power stations typically output a constant rate of power, they're now stuck in a cycle of too much power being requested when the sun is away and not enough power being requested to avoid wastage when it's sunny. This is leading to a lot of coal power stations being forced to soon shut down as they can't ensure a stable income to support and maintain their operations. Overall, the cost of coal power is rising, coal power stations are shutting down and renewables are becoming cheaper & more common. While coal was previously the most common power source in Australia and as a result a cheaper one, the stigma around climate change is sucessfully pushing Australia towards a more renewable future in the energy industry.
